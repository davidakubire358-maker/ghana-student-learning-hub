Study smarter

Study tips

Small, consistent habits make learning easier.

🗓️ Make a simple timetable

Set realistic study periods for each subject. Include short breaks and time to review what you learned.

🧠 Use active recall

Close your book and explain the idea in your own words. Then check what you missed.

✍️ Practise regularly

Try a few questions often instead of waiting until the day before an examination.

📖 Read with a purpose

Preview headings, note key points, look up unfamiliar words and summarise each section.

👥 Ask for help

Discuss difficult topics with a teacher, classmate or trusted adult. Explain your thinking clearly.

🌙 Protect your rest

Plan ahead so you can sleep well. A rested mind helps with attention and memory.
